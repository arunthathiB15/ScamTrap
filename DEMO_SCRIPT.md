# ScamTrap demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

Before recording: open your live page once so the free server is awake. Record the screen with your voice.

1. **Problem (20s).** "Scam messages in India, like fake KYC and digital arrest calls, push people to act in minutes. Most tools only say yes or no. ScamTrap explains why, and fights back."
2. **Check a message (40s).** Click "Bank KYC example", then Check message. Point out the verdict and score, the quoted warning signs, the closest known scam, and the what-to-do list.
3. **Complaint (15s).** Show the complaint draft and click Copy. "It is ready to paste into cybercrime.gov.in."
4. **Decoy (50s).** Click "Run decoy demo". "A fictional, confused victim keeps the scammer talking. In this demo the scammer is a built-in simulation, never a real person." Show the details revealed (UPI ID, phone, account) and the evidence pack.
5. **Safe message (10s).** Click "Normal message" and Check. "It does not cry wolf on normal messages."
6. **Honesty and close (15s).** "Rules plus retrieval, with an optional LLM. 28 of 30 on a held-out test set. Limits are listed in the README." Show the repo.
